# Image Setup Instructions

Please copy your brand assets to this folder:

## Required Images

1. **logo.png** - Your Dawai logo (the one with "দাওয়াই" text and medical cross/pill/leaf design)
   - Recommended size: 300x150px or similar aspect ratio
   - Format: PNG with transparent background preferred
   
2. **hero-banner.jpg** - Your hero image (pharmacist and delivery person with Shaheed Minar)
   - Recommended size: 1920x800px for best quality on desktop
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   
3. **favicon.ico** - Browser tab icon
   - You can convert your logo to .ico format using an online tool
   - Recommended size: 32x32px or 48x48px

## How to Add Images

Simply copy these files from your uploads to:
```
dawai-website/public/
```

The website will automatically use them once they're in place.
